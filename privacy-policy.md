**Spell Helper: Caster Companion** (the "app"), by Fall In Studio. In force from 28 October 2026.

## In short

The app collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

## What is stored, and where

Your characters, their spells, slots and resources, the journal, your settings and the content packs you import are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Only the app can read them there.

## Files you choose to move

- **Backups.** When you make a backup, the app writes a file and hands it to the Android share window. Where it goes next — a folder, a cloud drive, a message — is your choice, and from then on the app or service you picked handles it under its own terms.
- **Content packs and backups you open.** The app reads only the file you pick in the Android file picker, and only to import it.

## Android backup

If backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, as it does for other apps, and restore it on a new device. Google handles that copy under its own privacy policy; we never receive it. You can turn it off in your device's settings.

## Deleting your data

Delete characters and packs in the app, clear the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app. Each removes the data from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this policy is updated first, with a new date at the top.
