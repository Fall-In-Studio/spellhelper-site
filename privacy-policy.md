**Spell Helper: Caster Companion** (the "app"), by Fall In Studio. In force from 19 November 2026.

## In short

Until you use the online party, the app collects no personal data: everything you enter stays on your device, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It never asks for your name or e-mail, and you need no account.

The online party is the one feature that uses our server: it connects the phones of one party at the table. The server then keeps only what the party needs to work, as described below, on our own servers in Poland, in the EU.

## What is stored, and where

Your characters, their spells, slots and resources, the journal, your settings and the content packs you import are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Only the app can read them there. The online party does not change that: your character sheets stay on your phone, and the server never reads them.

## Online party

This section describes the online party feature as a whole. Some parts of it (portraits in the party, the shared initiative order, hidden entries, who is at the table, the buttons in Settings) arrive in later versions of the app; what is said here applies to them from the day they do.

### What our server keeps

- **Your phone's party key.** The first time you create or join a party, the server gives the app a random number for your phone and a random secret. The secret stays on your phone. The server keeps only a scrambled form of it (a hash) that cannot be turned back into the secret, with when the key was made and last used.
- **The parties you are in.** For each party: its name, chosen by the host, and who is in it: whether each member is the host or a guest, a player or the game master, and when they joined.
- **Your characters in the party, as the others see them:** each character's name and, if you have given it one, its portrait. Nothing else from the character: not its sheet, hit points, spells or journal. A game master without a character is shown as "Game master". The other members see your characters only by name and portrait, and you see theirs the same way.
- **Portraits.** If a character in the party has a portrait, the app sends a copy of it to the server, so that the other members' phones can show it. The server checks only its size and format. It deletes the portrait a few days after you leave the table, and at once when you leave the party or delete your data.
- **Invitations.** The host's invitation link, QR code and short code, kept only in a scrambled form, with when they expire (after 7 days) and how often they were used.
- **The party's game at the table:** the shared initiative order with the characters, enemies and other entries in it, their rolls, the round and whose turn it is. An entry the game master hides is sent only to the game master (in a party without one, only to whoever added it). It reaches the other players' phones only when it is revealed.
- **For the host only: the licence.** Hosting an online party is a paid feature, so the server asks Google Play whether the host's purchase is valid and keeps the answer: which product, a reference to the purchase, whether it is for life or a subscription, until when it is valid, and when it was checked. Guests buy nothing, and the server checks nothing about them.

### What our server does not keep

- **Reactions.** Thanks, warnings and emotes go to the others' phones as short codes, so that each phone shows them in its own language. They are shown for a few seconds and are not stored.
- **Who is at the table** is known only while your phone is connected.
- No e-mail address, no real name, no account, no contacts, no location and no advertising ID.

### Internet addresses

Like every server, ours sees the internet address (IP address) of the phone that connects to it. It uses the address for two things only:

- to stop anyone from guessing invitation codes or flooding the server: tries from one address are counted in the server's memory for a minute, and failed tries to join under a scrambled form of the address for one day;
- in the server's technical logs, which record each connection with its address and time, so that faults and attacks can be found. The logs are deleted after 14 days, and the more detailed technical traces after 72 hours.

The logs and traces stay on our own servers. They are never used to find out who you are, to follow what you do in the app, or for advertising.

### Why, and on what legal basis

We keep this data only to run the online party: to let your phone into your party, show the members to each other, keep the party's game the same on every phone, and check the host's licence. That is the service you ask for when you use the feature (GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)). Internet addresses in the logs and limits protect the server and its players from abuse, which is our legitimate interest (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)).

### Where

On our own servers in Poland, in the European Union. Fall In Studio runs them itself; no other company stores the party's data or copies of it.

### Who else takes part

- **Cloudflare** carries the connection between the app and our server and protects the server from attacks. On the way it handles what the app sends and sees your phone's internet address, but it does not keep the party's data. Cloudflare does this for us under its data processing terms. Where its network handles the connection outside the EU, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
- **Google Play** handles the host's purchase under Google's own privacy policy. Our server only asks Google Play whether a purchase is valid.

We do not sell the data or give it to anyone else.

### How long

- A party, its members, their characters in it, its invitations and its game: until a member leaves (their part is deleted at once), the host removes a member, or the host dissolves the party (all of it is deleted at once).
- Portraits: a few days after their owner leaves the table; at once when the owner leaves the party.
- When the host's licence ends, the party stops working 7 days later but is kept, so that it can start again when the licence is renewed. A party that has not worked for 12 months is deleted.
- Your phone's party key: deleted when it has not been used for 12 months.
- All of it, the host's licence record included: deleted at once when you delete your data (see below).
- Counts of failed tries to join: one day. Technical logs: 14 days. Technical traces: 72 hours.

## Files you choose to move

- **Backups.** When you make a backup, the app writes a file and hands it to the Android share window. Where it goes next — a folder, a cloud drive, a message — is your choice, and from then on the app or service you picked handles it under its own terms.
- **Content packs and backups you open.** The app reads only the file you pick in the Android file picker, and only to import it.

## Android backup

If backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, as it does for other apps, and restore it on a new device. Google handles that copy under its own privacy policy; we never receive it. You can turn it off in your device's settings. Your phone's party key is never part of that copy, nor of the app's own backups.

## Deleting your data

Delete characters and packs in the app, clear the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app. Each removes the data from your device.

For the online party:

- **Leave a party** in the app, or **Leave all parties** in Settings: you, your characters and their portraits in those parties are deleted from the server at once. Your phone's party key and the licence stay, so you can join or host a party again.
- **Dissolve a party** (the host): the party, its members, invitations, game and portraits are deleted from the server at once.
- **Delete my data** in Settings: all your online data is deleted from the server at once: your phone's party key, the licence record, your place in every party, and the invitations, entries and portraits you added. Parties you host are dissolved with it.
- **If you can no longer use the app** (for example you have uninstalled it), the party key on the server can no longer be used, and it is deleted after 12 months without use. To have everything deleted sooner, write to us at the address below, and we delete it within a month. We may ask for the name of your party or of your characters, to find it.

You may also ask us what we keep about you, ask us to correct it, to limit or stop its use, or to give you a copy, by writing to the same address. You may also complain to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland.
